To use "Tum Jo Aaye" (or any other song) as the background music:

1. Get a legal copy of the mp3 (e.g. export it from a music app you're
   subscribed to, or purchase it — I can't provide copyrighted audio files).
2. Rename it to:  tum-jo-aaye.mp3
3. Put it in this folder, right next to this README.

That's it — script.js already looks for music/tum-jo-aaye.mp3 and will play
it on loop once she taps "Yes". If the file isn't here, the page falls back
to a soft synthesized tune automatically, so nothing breaks either way.
